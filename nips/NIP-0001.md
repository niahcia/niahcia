# NIP-0001: NIAHCIA Protocol Principles

- Status: Draft
- Type: Meta
- Created: 2026-09-28

## Abstract

This NIP establishes the foundational architectural principles of NIAHCIA.

## Principles

1. The blockchain remains functional without the AI network.
2. AI compute remains optional for miners and full nodes.
3. CPU miners, compute workers, and service nodes have independent economic roles.
4. Agents are portable network identities rather than individual servers.
5. No centrally operated scheduler, storage host, inference API, or website may be required for protocol operation.
6. EVM smart contracts are a first-class application environment.
7. Large AI data and realtime streams remain off-chain while commitments and settlement are anchored on-chain.
8. Protocol objects are versioned and content-addressable where practical.
9. Verification mechanisms must be replaceable and may evolve from redundant execution to optimistic or cryptographic schemes.
10. Protocol v1 should future-proof object models and capability interfaces even when early implementations support only a subset.
11. The official website is a primary user experience, not a protocol authority.
12. Network services are rewarded for measurable service rather than ownership of collateral alone.

## Rationale

These principles preserve decentralization while allowing the AI, storage, runtime, and verification layers to evolve independently from base-chain consensus.

## Security considerations

Violating these boundaries risks introducing centralized dependencies, consensus coupling, or economic capture. Future NIPs should explicitly identify any proposed exception to these principles.
